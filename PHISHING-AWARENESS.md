## What is Social Engineering?
Social engineering is when attackers trick people (not computers) to get secret information. They use psychology, not hacking tools.

---

## Common Attack Types

### 1. Phishing Email
**What:** Fake email that looks real
**Example:** "Your account will be deleted! Click here to login" → fake website
**How to spot:**
- Sender email is wrong (support@g00gle.com)
- Link URL is different from real site
- Creates urgency or fear

### 2. Spear Phishing
**What:** Targeted phishing using your personal info
**Example:** "Dear Sufian, your Developers Hub account needs verification"
**How to spot:**
- Uses your real name but wrong details
- Asks for password or OTP

### 3. Vishing (Voice Phishing)
**What:** Fake phone call from "bank" or "IT support"
**Example:** "Sir your account is hacked, give us your OTP"
**How to spot:**
- Real banks never ask OTP on call
- Pressure to act fast

### 4. Pretexting
**What:** Attacker pretends to be someone else
**Example:** Pretends to be IT support to get your password
**How to spot:**
- Verify identity before sharing anything
- Call back on official number

## Protection Measures

✅ Never share password or OTP with anyone
✅ Always check email sender address carefully
✅ Hover over links before clicking
✅ Use 2-Factor Authentication (2FA)
✅ Report suspicious emails to IT team
✅ When in doubt, call back on official number

---

## Conclusion
Social engineering attacks target humans, not systems. The best defense is awareness and training. Always verify before trusting.
